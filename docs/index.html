01 / System

Dense radar sensing

Four cascaded chips and Doppler-division multiple access (DDMA) bring fine angular resolution to human point-cloud sensing.

Simultaneous transmissions are separated in the Doppler domain. On-board range–Doppler processing, channel calibration, target detection and angle estimation produce 5D points: position, Doppler and SNR.

04 / Method

Part-Aware Spatio-Temporal Network

PAST-Net reorganizes radar features into skeleton-aware part tokens and models their temporal evolution to predict the target-frame 3D pose.

01

Frame-level features

A point-cloud encoder extracts each frame’s global representation from 5D radar attributes.

02

Skeleton-aware tokens

Spatial modeling organizes frame features around human body parts.

03

Part-wise attention

Temporal self-attention follows each part across consecutive observations.

04

3D pose regression

Global and part-level features fuse to locate all 19 joints in the target frame.
